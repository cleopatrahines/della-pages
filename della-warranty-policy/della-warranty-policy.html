Warranty FAQs

Which mini split brands offer the best warranty coverage compared to Mitsubishi and Daikin?

Compare what each warranty requires, not just the number of years. Mitsubishi Electric's longest residential term, 12 years on parts and compressor, requires installation by a Diamond Contractor® or Ductless Pro and registration within 90 days of installation. Daikin's 12-year parts warranty requires online registration within 60 days of installation. DELLA mini splits registered within 100 days of purchase get a lifetime warranty on parts, including the compressor, for DIY and professional installations alike.

Does DIY installation void the DELLA mini split warranty?

No. Registered DIY installs get the same free replacement parts as professional installs. If you used a DELLA rental kit, add a link to your installation video when you register. Damage caused by improper installation is not covered, so follow the installation manual.

What does "lifetime" mean?

Coverage lasts for as long as you, the original purchaser, own the mini split and it remains installed at the address you registered. It covers replacement parts for confirmed defects, including the compressor. After the first year, you pay shipping.

What if I miss the 100-day registration window?

The standard 1-year limited warranty still applies. After the first year, an unregistered mini split has no parts coverage, so you pay for parts and shipping.

Does the warranty cover labor?

No. Technician labor, diagnosis and installation costs are not covered. For professional installs, if the unit still malfunctions after parts are replaced and you have a licensed technician's defect report, DELLA will take care of you at its discretion.

Is the warranty transferable?

No. The extended warranty stays with the original purchaser and the registered installation address.
