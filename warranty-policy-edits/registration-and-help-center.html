What is the warranty policy for the DELLA mini split?

Every DELLA mini split comes with a 1-year limited warranty. Register within 100 days of purchase and DELLA adds complimentary lifetime parts coverage, including the compressor, for as long as the original purchaser owns the system and it remains installed at the registered address. Lifetime coverage applies to both DIY and professional installations.

1. Returns & Warranty Coverage Within 30 Days of Purchase

Full Return: DELLA provides comprehensive assistance for all orders within 30 days of purchase. Our money-back guarantee allows you to return the item first, after which we will process your refund. Products must be returned in their original operating condition, including all accessories and packaging.

Parts Replacement: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts free of charge. DELLA will also cover all shipping expenses for the delivery of replacement parts.

2. Warranty Coverage from 31 Days to 1 Year

Registered Product and DIY (Non-professional) Install: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts free of charge. DELLA will also cover all shipping expenses for the delivery of replacement parts.

Registered Product and Professional Install: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts free of charge. DELLA will also cover all shipping expenses for the delivery of replacement parts. If, after parts are replaced, the customer's unit is still malfunctioning and the customer has a licensed HVAC technician's defect report, DELLA will take care of the customer at its discretion.

Unregistered Product: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts. DELLA will also cover all shipping expenses for the delivery of replacement parts.

3. Extended Lifetime Warranty (Beyond 1 Year)

Extended Warranty Program: Customers who register their mini split unit within 100 days of purchase will receive complimentary lifetime parts coverage, including the compressor. This applies whether the system was installed by the customer or by a licensed HVAC technician.

Registered Product and DIY (Non-professional) Install: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts free of charge. The customer is responsible for all shipping costs.

Registered Product and Professional Install: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts free of charge. If, after parts are replaced, the customer's unit is still malfunctioning and the customer has a licensed HVAC technician's defect report, DELLA will take care of the customer at its discretion. In any event, the customer is responsible for all shipping costs.

Unregistered Product: If a product defect is confirmed, DELLA will, at its discretion, provide the necessary replacement parts. The customer is responsible for the costs of all parts and shipping.

4. Warranty Registration

You can register it here: https://dellahome.com/pages/warranty. Register within 100 days of purchase. If you installed with a DELLA rental kit, include a link to your installation video to activate the lifetime warranty. For a professional install, include the installer's name and HVAC license number.

Not covered: technician labor, diagnosis and installation costs, refrigerant, damage from improper installation or use, third-party parts, electrical failures and environmental damage. See the full Warranty Policy.
