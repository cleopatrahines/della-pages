WARRANTY POLICY

Maximizing Savings with Energy-Efficient HVAC Systems

Homeowners across the U.S. can take advantage of valuable federal, state, and local tax credits or rebates when they invest in energy-efficient HVAC systems, such as mini splits, heat pumps, or air conditioners. These financial incentives are designed to encourage the adoption of environmentally friendly technologies, which help reduce energy consumption and lower utility bills.

By installing systems that meet ENERGY STAR® standards or other qualifying criteria, homeowners may be eligible to receive rebates at both the state and local levels, and even claim federal tax credits. These programs not only ease the initial cost of purchasing energy-efficient units but also contribute to long-term energy savings and environmental benefits.

Eligibility: Your air conditioning must meet or exceed the ENERGY STAR® requirements for efficiency. This usually involves specific Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio (SEER) and Heating Seasonal Performance Factor (HSPF) ratings.

Standard Warranty

All our products come with a standard warranty, and no registration is required: 1 year for mini splits, pool heat pumps, window ACs, portable ACs and tower fans, and 3 years on parts and 5 years on the compressor for central AC. Full terms are also in your user manual.

Extended Warranty

Don't forget: we also offer a free extended warranty. So, sign up today for more peace of mind!

To qualify for the free extended warranty, the unit must meet the following conditions:

  • Be registered within 100 days of purchase (the date on your order).

  • Mini splits installed with a DELLA rental kit: include a link to your installation video when you register to activate the lifetime warranty.
  • Professionally installed systems: include the installer's name and HVAC license number when you register.

Warranty Parts Replacement

Some parts may need to be returned for replacement. The limited lifetime warranty covers replacement parts for confirmed defects, including the compressor on registered mini splits. It does not cover labor or the costs listed below. For more information, please contact us.

Not Covered by Warranty

  1. Technician Labor: Costs for any technician services such as examining, disassembling, reassembling, uninstalling, or reinstalling the unit.
  2. Parts & Labor for Repairs: Costs related to diagnosing, repairing, removing, installing, shipping, or handling defective or new parts, including routine maintenance.
  3. Usage Costs: Expenses for electricity, fuel, refrigerant replacement, refilling, or disposal.
  4. Damage from Improper Use: Damage caused by improper installation, abnormal or incorrect usage, incorrect servicing, modifications, or using the product in ways not intended by the manufacturer.
  5. Third-Party Parts: Any parts not supplied or approved by DELLA.
  6. Electrical Failures: Failures due to voltage issues, including improper voltage installation, open circuit breakers, blown fuses, or electrical outages.
  7. Environmental Damage: Damage from natural disasters or elements, such as rain, flooding, fire, lightning, corrosive chemicals, etc.
  8. Incidental or Consequential Damages: Depending on your state or province, some incidental or consequential damages may not be covered.

Warranty FAQs

Does DIY installation void the DELLA mini split warranty?

No. Registered DIY installs get the same free replacement parts as professional installs. If you used a DELLA rental kit, add a link to your installation video when you register. Damage caused by improper installation is not covered, so follow the installation manual.

What does "lifetime" mean?

Coverage lasts for as long as you, the original purchaser, own the mini split and it remains installed at the address you registered. It covers replacement parts for confirmed defects, including the compressor. After the first year, you pay shipping.

What if I miss the 100-day registration window?

The standard 1-year limited warranty still applies. After the first year, an unregistered mini split has no parts coverage, so you pay for parts and shipping.

Does the warranty cover labor?

No. Technician labor, diagnosis and installation costs are not covered. For professional installs, if the unit still malfunctions after parts are replaced and you have a licensed technician's defect report, DELLA will take care of you at its discretion.

Is the warranty transferable?

No. The extended warranty stays with the original purchaser and the registered installation address.
