Before you choose 12K or 18K

Is 12,000 BTU or 18,000 BTU better for my room?

Start with the room's boundaries and load, not the bigger number. A 12,000 BTU mini split is often the better first look for a smaller enclosed room. An 18,000 BTU mini split is worth checking when the space is larger, open to other rooms, strongly sun-exposed, or used in a way that adds heat. Final sizing should still be confirmed with a qualified installer.

How many square feet can a 12,000 BTU mini split cover?

Della 12,000 BTU models are commonly referenced for enclosed rooms up to 550 sq. ft. They make the most sense for bedrooms, offices, nurseries, and smaller living rooms with normal ceilings and moderate sun. If that room opens into a hallway, kitchen, garage, or sunroom, do not choose by square footage alone.

How many square feet can an 18,000 BTU mini split cover?

Della 18,000 BTU models are commonly referenced for larger or more open rooms up to 1,000 sq. ft. Treat that as a shopping reference, not a promise. A bright sunroom, tall-ceiling living area, or garage can behave very differently from an insulated interior room with the same footprint.

Is bigger always safer when choosing between 12K and 18K?

No. Bigger can feel safer on paper, but an oversized system may run in short bursts instead of delivering steady comfort. If the room is smaller and enclosed, 12K may be the smarter path. If the room is larger, open, sunny, or high-load, 18K is worth checking with the full room conditions in mind.

Should I choose 18K for a garage, sunroom, or open living area?

Usually, yes, it is worth checking the 18K path for those spaces. The issue is often not just square footage; it is sun, insulation, air volume, and stored heat. A garage or sunroom may still need installer review, but 18K is the more realistic collection to compare first for many of those rooms.
