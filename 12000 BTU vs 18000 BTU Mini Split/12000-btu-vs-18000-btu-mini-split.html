Before you choose 12K or 18K

Is 12,000 BTU or 18,000 BTU better for my room?

Start with the room itself, not the bigger number. Choose 12K for a smaller enclosed room; check 18K when the space is open, sunny, high-ceiling, or harder to cool.

How many square feet can a 12,000 BTU mini split cover?

A 12,000 BTU mini split is commonly used as a reference for enclosed rooms up to 550 sq. ft. If the room opens into another area or gets strong sun, do not size by square footage alone.

How many square feet can an 18,000 BTU mini split cover?

An 18,000 BTU mini split is commonly used as a reference for larger or more open rooms up to 1,000 sq. ft. It is a stronger starting point, but the room layout and heat load still matter.

Is bigger always safer when choosing between 12K and 18K?

No. If the room is smaller and enclosed, 18K may be more capacity than the space needs; if it is open, sunny, or high-load, 18K is worth checking.

Should I choose 18K for a garage, sunroom, or open living area?

Usually, start by checking 18K for those spaces. Garages, sunrooms, and open layouts often need more capacity because of sun, insulation, air volume, and stored heat.
