Attic Mini Split FAQs

What size mini split does my attic need?
Size the system from a room-specific heating and cooling load calculation, not floor area alone. Attic insulation, air leakage, windows, sun exposure and ceiling height affect the load. Ask your installer to confirm capacity before choosing a model; the products shown here are examples, not a sizing recommendation.
Can the indoor and outdoor units both go in the attic?
The outdoor condenser belongs outdoors. A concealed ducted indoor unit may be installed in suitable attic roof space when its model-specific clearance, structural support, ductwork, drainage and service-access requirements can be met. Wall-mounted and ceiling-cassette units serve the finished room from their respective mounting positions.
Do I need single-zone or multi-zone for my attic?
A single-zone system may suit one open finished attic area. Separate rooms or different temperature needs may call for multiple zones. A ducted system can also serve multiple rooms when the duct layout is designed for them. Have your installer assess each room’s load, airflow and control needs before selecting a configuration.
What affects the total cost of an attic mini split?
The displayed product prices cover equipment, not the complete installed project. Labor, electrical work, line sets, condensate drainage, any ductwork and access or finish repairs can add to the cost. Check what the selected product includes and request an itemized installation quote for your attic.
