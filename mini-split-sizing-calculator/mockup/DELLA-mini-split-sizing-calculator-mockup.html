Mini Split BTU Calculator: What Size Mini Split Do I Need?

For most single rooms, the answer lands between 9,000 and 24,000 BTU. A quick rule of thumb is about 20 BTU per square foot: a 400 sq. ft. bedroom starts around 9,000 BTU, a 500 sq. ft. office around 12,000, and an open living area near 1,000 sq. ft. around 18,000. But square footage is only the starting point. Climate region, ceiling height, insulation, sun exposure, and how many people use the room each move the answer, and together they can shift it a full size up or down. The calculator above starts from your square footage, then adjusts the HVAC load for the conditions of your actual room.

What the coverage label leaves out

A unit marked “up to 550 sq. ft.” assumes a typical room with 8-foot ceilings and decent insulation. The same 550 sq. ft. with a vaulted ceiling and strong afternoon sun can genuinely need the next size up, while a shaded, well-insulated room stays comfortable with less. When your calculated load and a product label disagree, trust the load.

Why a bigger mini split is not safer

Both sizing mistakes cost you. An undersized unit runs flat out through the hottest afternoons and never quite catches up. An oversized one cools the air so fast that it shuts off before pulling moisture out, leaves the room cold but clammy, then restarts a few minutes later. Installers call this short cycling; it wears the compressor and wastes electricity. Inverter technology softens the swings, but it cannot rescue a badly oversized system. Stay within about 15% of your calculated load.

Sizing a mini split for more than one room

Size each room on its own. A wall unit cannot cool through closed doors or around corners, so adding your rooms into one total and buying a single bigger unit does not work. Each room that needs its own comfort gets its own indoor head, and a multi-zone system runs up to six of them from one outdoor unit. Run the calculator once per room and match the combination to a pre-configured bundle.

When to double-check before you order

Sunrooms and glass-heavy spaces gain far more solar heat than a normal room, and vaulted ceilings add air volume the eye underestimates. If a mini split will be your primary heat in a cold-winter state, the outdoor temperature rating matters as much as BTU; DELLA’s cold-climate series are rated down to −13°F and −22°F. In these cases the calculator still gives you an honest starting point, but it flags the result so you can confirm with our team or a local installer before ordering.

Mini Split Sizing Calculator FAQs

How accurate is this mini split calculator?

It uses the same starting factors a professional would: room area, climate, ceiling height, insulation, sun, glass, and internal heat. And it shows a range instead of a single falsely precise number. It is not a Manual J calculation, the professional standard that models wall assemblies, window specs, and local design weather in detail. For choosing a single-room unit it is reliable. For whole-home projects or permit paperwork, hire a pro.

Should I get a 115V or 230V mini split?

9K and 12K sizes come in both voltages; larger systems are 230V only. 115V runs on a standard household circuit, which is why bedrooms and DIY installs favor it. 230V needs a dedicated double-pole breaker, like a dryer or range. Check your panel before ordering, and confirm the exact voltage and breaker requirement on the product page, because it varies by model.

Can I install a mini split myself?

Many DELLA customers do. Mounting the indoor head, setting the outdoor unit, and routing the line set are manageable weekend work with basic tools, and DELLA offers a rental tool kit with the vacuum pump and gauges the refrigerant connection requires. The electrical hookup is where most DIYers bring in a licensed electrician. Installing it yourself does not void the warranty as long as you register your system and follow the manual.

Will a mini split heat in winter?

Yes. Every DELLA mini split is a heat pump that heats and cools. The difference is how cold it can keep working outside: standard series are rated to 5°F, and the cold-climate series run down to −13°F or −22°F. Enter your ZIP Code above and the calculator only suggests series rated for your winters. In the coldest regions, keep a backup heat source for the deepest cold snaps.

What size mini split for an open floor plan?

If the space flows together through wide openings, measure it as one and enter the total square footage. If doors close between areas, or a corner blocks the airflow, treat each area as its own room; every room gets its own indoor unit, ideally on one multi-zone system. Past roughly 1,500 sq. ft., plan on two indoor units for even airflow no matter what the math says. Open stairways to another floor are the trickiest case. Size generously there and check with our support team before you order.

How much does a mini split cost to run?

Less than almost any other electric heating or cooling. Efficiency is measured in SEER2, and DELLA systems range from 17 to 25; a higher number means less electricity for the same comfort. Your actual bill depends on your climate, electric rate, and running hours, but a right-sized inverter unit costs far less to run than window units, portable ACs, or electric baseboard heat.
