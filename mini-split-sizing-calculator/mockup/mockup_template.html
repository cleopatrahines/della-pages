What changes your mini split BTU requirement?
  • Climate — your ZIP Code adjusts the load −10% to +10% and filters which series can heat your winters.
  • Ceiling height — more air volume, more load: −10% to +50% versus the 8-foot standard.
  • Insulation and air tightness — a leaky, uninsulated room can need 30% more than a modern one.
  • Sun and glass — strong western sun and glass-heavy walls both add real load, counted separately.
  • People, kitchen, equipment — each extra person adds 600 BTU/h, a kitchen 4,000, devices their wattage.
Sizing single-zone and multi-zone mini splits

One room needs one single-zone system, from 9,000 to 36,000 BTU. Several rooms usually share one outdoor unit: size each room separately with the calculator above, then match the combination to a pre-configured DELLA multi-zone bundle — two to six indoor heads, up to 55,000 BTU. Never size a multi-room project by total square footage alone.

How this differs from a Manual J HVAC load calculation

This tool is a practical pre-purchase estimate. It considers the key drivers of room cooling load and tells you how confident the estimate is. It is not a substitute for ACCA Manual J, the professional standard that models wall assemblies, window performance, infiltration, and local design weather in detail.

For most single rooms, this estimate plus the built-in safety rules is enough to choose confidently. For whole-home projects, complex spaces, or code documentation, have a qualified HVAC professional run a full load calculation.

Mini Split Sizing Calculator FAQs

How accurate is this mini split calculator?

It uses the same room factors professionals start with — area, climate, ceiling height, insulation, sun, glass, and internal heat — and shows an estimated range with a confidence level. Complex spaces are flagged for review instead of forced into a single number.

Is this the same as an HVAC load calculator?

It provides a practical pre-purchase estimate of your room’s HVAC cooling load. It does not replace a professional Manual J calculation for complex or whole-home projects.

Should I size up to be safe?

Usually no. An oversized mini split short-cycles: it cools fast but removes less humidity and wastes energy. We recommend at most 15% above your estimated load.

Can I calculate multiple rooms?

Yes — run the calculator for each room separately, then match the combination to a pre-configured DELLA multi-zone bundle, up to six rooms and 55,000 BTU.

Does my climate affect mini split size?

Twice. Hot-humid climates raise the cooling load, and cold winters narrow which DELLA series can heat reliably. Your ZIP Code handles both automatically.

What if my result falls between two sizes?

You’ll see both paths and the conditions that favor each — for example, take the larger size with strong western sun or frequent guests. Never a falsely precise single answer.
