What DIY usually means — know your boundaries

Plan yourself

  • Research your model and space.
  • Plan line-set route, drain, and clearances.
  • Follow the manual for planning and prep steps.

Confirm before checkout

  • Verify voltage, panel capacity, and outlet location.
  • Confirm line-set length and route match your plan.
  • Know what is included and what requires pro input.

Use qualified help

  • Electrical, permits, and complex routing may require a pro.
  • When in doubt, get qualified HVAC support.
  • Safety and code compliance come first.

Frequently asked questions

Can I install a mini split myself?

Some planning and mounting preparation may be DIY-friendly for experienced homeowners. Electrical work, refrigerant-related work, permits, and final commissioning may require a licensed or certified professional depending on your location and exact system.

What tools do I need for a DIY mini split installation?

Common planning tools include measuring tools, a level, drill, mounting hardware, and hand tools. Some setups may call for specialized tools such as a vacuum pump, torque tools, or gauges, so review your model manual and consider the rental install kit.

Do I need a vacuum pump for a mini split?

Requirements depend on the exact model and installation method. Use the model manual and qualified technician support where certification or specialized setup work is required.

How do I choose the right line set?

Confirm the length, size, route, insulation, and compatibility with your exact Della model. The line set should match the planned route without being treated as a universal one-size-fits-all part.

What should I check before ordering a mini split?

Confirm sizing, voltage, panel capacity, line-set route, drain route, outdoor placement, permits or HOA rules, airflow, and the exact model manual before you order.

Does DIY installation affect warranty or support?

Do not assume warranty or support terms without checking the exact Della policy for your model. Review the product manual and contact Della support if you are unsure before installation.
