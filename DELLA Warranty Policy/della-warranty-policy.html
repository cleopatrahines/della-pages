What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty covers parts that need replacement. Knowing the exclusions up front keeps a claim simple.

Not covered

  • Technician laborExamining, disassembling, reassembling, uninstalling or reinstalling the unit
  • Repair costsDiagnosing, repairing, removing, installing, shipping or handling parts, and routine maintenance
  • Usage costsElectricity, fuel, refrigerant replacement, refilling or disposal
  • Improper useImproper installation, incorrect servicing, modifications or abnormal use
  • Third-party partsAny parts not supplied or approved by DELLA
  • Electrical failuresImproper voltage, open breakers, blown fuses or outages
  • Environmental damageRain, flooding, fire, lightning or corrosive chemicals
  • Incidental damagesSome incidental or consequential damages, depending on your state or province

Keep your coverage

  • Keep the unit at the address you registered
  • Install to the manual and on the correct voltage
  • Use only DELLA-supplied or approved parts

DELLA Warranty FAQs

Straight answers on coverage, registration, installation and claims.

Still have questions?Talk to a DELLA expert, 24/7
Which mini split brands offer the best warranty coverage compared to Mitsubishi and Daikin?

Compare what each term requires, not only the number of years. Mitsubishi Electric publishes 12-year parts and compressor coverage for systems installed in a single-family home by a Diamond Contractor® or Ductless Pro and registered within 90 days of installation. Daikin publishes a 12-year parts limited warranty that requires online registration within 60 days of installation (not required in California or Québec). DELLA mini splits registered within 100 days of purchase get complimentary lifetime parts coverage, and DELLA's policy lists both DIY and professional installations. Labor is not covered by DELLA's warranty.

Does the DELLA mini split warranty cover DIY installation?

Yes. DELLA's policy lists registered products with non-professional installation: if a product defect is confirmed, DELLA provides the necessary replacement parts free of charge. Damage caused by improper installation is not covered, so follow the installation manual.

What does DELLA's lifetime mini split warranty cover?

It covers replacement parts for a confirmed product defect, for mini splits registered within 100 days of purchase. Beyond the first year the parts are free and the customer pays shipping. Technician labor, diagnosis, refrigerant and damage from improper installation or use are not covered.

What happens if I don't register my DELLA mini split within 100 days?

The standard warranty in your user manual still applies and no registration is needed for it. For the first year, DELLA provides replacement parts for a confirmed defect and covers shipping. After one year, an unregistered unit has no lifetime parts coverage: the customer pays for parts and shipping.

Does the DELLA warranty cover labor?

No. Technician labor, such as examining, disassembling, reassembling, uninstalling or reinstalling the unit, and the cost of diagnosing or installing parts are not covered. For professionally installed units, if the unit still malfunctions after parts are replaced and you have a licensed HVAC technician's defect report, DELLA will take care of the customer at its discretion.

Is the DELLA extended warranty transferable?

The free extended warranty requires the unit to be owned by the original purchaser and to remain installed and operated at the address provided during registration.

What is the warranty on a DELLA central air conditioner?

The standard warranty is 3 years on parts and 5 years on the compressor. Registering within 100 days of purchase extends it to 5 years on parts and 10 years on the compressor.

How do I file a DELLA warranty claim?

Contact DELLA customer service at (800) 863-4143 or on live chat with your model, order details and a description of the problem. If a defect is confirmed, DELLA ships the replacement part. Some parts may need to be returned, and after 100 days the original PCB must be returned for a PCB replacement.
