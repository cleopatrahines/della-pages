Ceiling Cassette vs Concealed Ducted Mini Split Questions

Still have questions?Talk to a DELLA expert, 24/7
Can one concealed ducted unit serve more than one room?

It can, when the system is designed with supply ducts and a suitable return-air path. That does not automatically mean independent temperature control in every room. Confirm the design with a qualified HVAC professional.

Does a ceiling cassette require a drop ceiling?

Not necessarily. It needs a suitable ceiling opening and enough above-ceiling space for the indoor unit, drainage, lines, and service access. Confirm the structure and product requirements before installation.

How should I size a ceiling cassette or concealed ducted mini split?

Start with a room-by-room load calculation—not square footage alone. Insulation, windows, sun exposure, ceiling height, and airflow design all matter; confirm the final system with a qualified HVAC professional.
