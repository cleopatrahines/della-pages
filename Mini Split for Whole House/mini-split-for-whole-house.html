Whole-House Mini Split FAQs

Straight answers on indoor units, outdoor units, wiring and air quality.

Still have questions?Talk to a DELLA expert, 24/7
Do I need an indoor unit in every room?

Not necessarily. An open area or a properly designed ducted zone may serve more than one room. Each closed room still needs a planned source of heating and cooling.

Can one outdoor unit serve my whole house?

It depends on your home’s heating and cooling loads, the approved indoor-unit combination and installation limits. Adding up the indoor units’ BTU ratings does not tell you how much the outdoor unit can deliver at once. Have your installer confirm the combination before you buy.

Will I need to upgrade my electrical panel?

It depends on your existing loads, available panel space and the selected equipment. A multi-zone system may need fewer dedicated supply circuits than separate single-zone systems, but that does not establish whether your panel has enough capacity. Have an electrician check the requirements for every outdoor system and any additional equipment before ordering.

What about fresh air, filtration, and humidity?

A mini split does not automatically supply fresh air or meet all filtration and humidity-control needs. Ask your installer whether your home needs additional equipment.
